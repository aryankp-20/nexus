01 — Platform Overview

Built for the statistical workforce, not a generic LMS.

Officers and statisticians across the Official Statistical System operate under precise role-based competency requirements. This platform identifies role, measures actual capability, and closes the gap with evidence — not enrollment counts.

Institutional by design

Built to the trust, security and reliability standards expected of a government intelligence system — not a consumer learning app.

Evidence-based competency

Every competency score is derived from diagnostic assessment performance, never self-reported skill claims.

Connected to iGOT Karmayogi

Competency gaps are mapped directly to relevant iGOT Karmayogi courses and CBPs for immediate action.
